Spice Route was born from a simple conviction: India's regional snacking traditions are being homogenised by mass production. Every region has its own flavour grammar — its own spice logic, its own textures, its own stories told through food.

We travel the routes that spices have travelled for centuries. We find the families who've been making these snacks for generations. And we bring their craft to your doorstep — unchanged, unprocessed, unapologetic.

We publish full batch-level ingredient lists for every product. No hidden fillers, no undeclared additives. Every compound, every source — openly available.

Allergen Crisis Response Protocol

In the event of any allergen contamination or mislabelling incident, Spice Route commits to the following immediate actions:

  1. Immediate Product Recall: Within 4 hours of identification, all affected batches are pulled from distribution. No exceptions, no delays.
  2. Public Notification: Affected customers are contacted directly via email and SMS within 6 hours. A public notice is posted on our website and social media channels simultaneously.
  3. Independent Investigation: An independent food safety laboratory is engaged within 24 hours to determine the root cause.
  4. Full Transparency Report: A complete incident report is published within 7 days, including root cause analysis, corrective actions, and preventive measures.
  5. Affected Customer Support: Full refunds, replacement products, and if applicable, coverage of medical consultation costs for any allergic reactions.

This protocol is maintained by our Quality Assurance team and reviewed quarterly. Last review: March 2026.
