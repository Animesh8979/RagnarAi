# Spice Route: AIMC Project Submission & Methodology

**Course:** AI Marketing Course (AIMC)  
**Project:** Premium Regional Indian Snacks (Spice Route)  
**Team Members:** Animesh Shukla | Anhad Vikal | Yogesh Rajput

---

## Executive Summary of AI Integration
This project demonstrates the end-to-end application of Artificial Intelligence across the entire digital marketing spectrum—spanning creative generation, sentiment analysis, predictive modeling, and autonomous web deployment. By strategically combining human curation with AI generation, we developed a comprehensive, enterprise-grade brand ecosystem for *Spice Route*.

---

### Mission 1 — Social Media Campaign
- **Execution:** Engineered a detailed brand voice document and prompt sequence within **Claude AI** to generate a culturally specific content calendar.
- **Curation:** Reviewed and edited all AI-generated entries to ensure festival accuracy and regional authenticity. 
- **Creative:** Designed visual posts using **Canva AI** capabilities.
- **Outcome:** Finalized ad copy for two distinct target segments and published the campaign live on an operations-ready Instagram account.

### Mission 2 — Social Listening and Competitive Intelligence
- **Execution:** Configured a **Brand24** monitoring platform with crisis-specific keyword clusters.
- **Data Analysis:** Exported social data into **Julius AI** to run advanced sentiment analysis.
- **Benchmarking:** Compiled comprehensive competitor data using **BuzzSumo**.
- **Outcome:** Synthesized the data into a dynamic **Looker Studio** social intelligence dashboard, authored a strategic founder’s letter, and produced the final competitive benchmarking report.

### Mission 3 — Crisis Management
- **Execution:** Designed a three-tier crisis severity framework to protect brand integrity.
- **Content generation:** Drafted crisis response templates dynamically using **Claude AI**, heavily editing the outputs to ensure warmth, brand voice, and specificity.
- **Simulation:** Authored a complete, hour-by-hour (Hour 0 to Hour 72) allergen crisis simulation.
- **Prototyping:** Built and recorded a live **Antigravity crisis alert prototype**, directing simulated alerts dynamically to authorized endpoints via WhatsApp.
- **Analysis:** Supplemented the simulation with a counterfactual analysis on Mamaearth.

### Mission 4 — Customer Analytics
- **Execution:** Designed the schema and generated a synthetic transaction dataset directly through **Claude AI**.
- **Data Analysis:** Processed the dataset through **Julius AI** to execute RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) segmentation.
- **Strategy:** Named and explicitly defined the resulting four customer segments, calculating CLV (Customer Lifetime Value) for each.
- **Outcome:** Centralized all consumer findings inside a customized **Looker Studio** analytics dashboard to drive localized marketing strategies.

### Mission 5 — Predictive Modelling
- **Execution:** Expanded our synthetic CRM dataset to 800 comprehensive records.
- **Machine Learning:** Uploaded the dataset to **Akkio** and extensively documented the AutoML training process, achieving a targeted model accuracy of **78.4%**.
- **Reporting:** Distilled complex model metrics into plain language via an Executive Note for stakeholders, along with a DPDPA ethical compliance analysis.
- **Prototyping:** Constructed an interactive prediction interface within **Replit** and documented it with an interface walkthrough recording.

### Mission 6 — Website and Chatbot Deployment
- **Deployment:** Architected and deployed a multi-page, dark-mode cinematic e-commerce environment via **GitHub Pages**.
- **Features Addressed:** Integrated an interactive SVG tracing the Indian spice route, dynamic preferences quiz, robust product catalogue, full cart/checkout state management, and strict allergen transparency modals.
- **AI Integration:** Programmed a custom **Tidio FAQ knowledge base** document. Configured the chatbot's decision tree algorithms and automated email capture endpoints.
- **Outcome:** Successfully deployed a fully functional live site, capped with a recorded 2-minute demonstration traversing the user journey.
